# LEVIA AI 🚀
### The Operating System for the Modern Content Creator

**Create More. Manage Less. Grow Smarter.**

## 🌟 About the Project

LEVIA AI is an AI-powered digital ecosystem designed to simplify the way modern creators, freelancers, and digital agencies manage their creative and business operations.

The platform brings multiple specialized workspaces together into one unified environment, helping users streamline content creation, manage projects and clients, organize advertising campaigns, and explore product intelligence.

Instead of switching between disconnected tools, LEVIA AI aims to provide a smarter, more organized, and connected digital working experience.

## 💡 Key Features

- **Creative Studio:** AI-assisted content ideation, script generation, personalized captions, and content preparation.
- **Freelancer Workspace:** Manage clients, projects, services, proposals, invoices, earnings, and schedules.
- **Agency Workspace:** Organize advertising campaigns, clients, creative assets, approvals, and campaign activities.
- **Beluga – Product Intelligence:** Explore products, discover prospects, analyze geographic opportunities, and manage follow-ups.
- **Smart Onboarding:** Personalize the user experience through guided onboarding and preference-based workflows.
- **Unified Dashboard:** Access specialized tools and workflows through one connected platform.

## 🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to reduce operational complexity through intelligent assistance, connected workflows, and user-focused automation.

LEVIA AI aims to empower creators and digital businesses to focus less on repetitive management tasks and more on creativity, productivity, and growth.
